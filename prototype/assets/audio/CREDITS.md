# Credits and licences

Everything in the game that was not made for it comes from free sources. All of it is **CC0 (public domain)** unless noted.

## Characters and animation
- **Hanuman 3D model and textures**: supplied by the project owner (Tripo-generated FBX, plus the gada from `hanuman.obj`).
  Rigged and skinned in Blender with the scripts in `tools/blender/`.
- **Animations**: [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com) Universal Animation Library 1 and 2 (CC0), retargeted onto Hanuman
  and the rakshasa with `tools/blender/b_retarget.py`. Source files via
  [Dallolz/moorfall-assets](https://github.com/Dallolz/moorfall-assets).
- **Rakshasa base body**: Quaternius Universal Base Characters, "Superhero Male" (CC0); horns, tusks, crown, jewellery and
  dhoti added in Blender (`tools/blender/b_rakshasa.py`).
- **Weapons**: Quaternius Modular Weapons Pack, Axe and Hammer (CC0); trishul and torch modelled in code.

## Environment
- **Props** (palms, bushes, grass, flowers, lilies, bamboo, rocks, market stalls, carts, barrels, boxes, urns, lanterns,
  fire baskets, banners, fountain): [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) Nature Kit, Fantasy Town Kit, Survival Kit and
  Graveyard Kit (CC0), via [shorepine/kenney](https://github.com/shorepine/kenney).
- **Stone textures**: [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) red sandstone wall and pavement, large sandstone blocks (CC0).

## Sound and music
- Footsteps and impacts: Kenney **Impact Sounds** (CC0), via
  [Boyquotes/kenney-impact-sounds-for-godot](https://github.com/Boyquotes/kenney-impact-sounds-for-godot).
- Whooshes, punches, sword clash, monster hurt and death cries, efforts, night, rain, wind and water ambience, bush rustle:
  **Meta Open Sound** (CC0), [facebookincubator/Meta-open-sound](https://github.com/facebookincubator/Meta-open-sound).
- Music ("Cave 4", "Cave 2", "Beginning 6" by **HoliznaCC0**), crickets, thunder, gong and roar:
  [seyedpouramir/alvana-audio](https://github.com/seyedpouramir/alvana-audio) (CC0).
- The tanpura drone is synthesised in the browser.
