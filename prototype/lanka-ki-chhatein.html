Lanka ki Chhatein

Lanka mein sandhya. Chhote roop mein Hanuman ko chhatton aur galiyon ke raaste, rakshason se bachte ya unse ladte hue, Ashok Vatika mein Sita Mata tak Rama ki mudrika pahunchani hai.

Ashok ke ped ke neeche Sita Mata ne Rama ki mudrika pehchaan li. Ye prototype yahin tak hai.

Hanuman, rakshas aur Lanka aa rahe hain… (lagbhag 17 MB)
